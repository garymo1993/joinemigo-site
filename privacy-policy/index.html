This Privacy Policy (also called our "Privacy Notice") for Emigo Wellness, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Emigo," "we," "us," or "our"), explains how we collect, use, disclose and otherwise process information in connection with Emigo. Emigo is designed to work with as little personal information as possible. Your everyday typing is not sent to us. Text or screenshots are processed only when you choose to use a feature such as Reframe, Interpret, Create, Reply, Ask Emigo, Chat or Unpack, and only to produce the result you asked for. Your messages are never stored or sold by Emigo. Your learned words, typing data and history live on your device.

We process your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Visit our website at joinemigo.com, or any website of ours that links to this Privacy Notice;
  • Download and use our mobile application (Emigo), including the Emigo keyboard, or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice;
  • Use Emigo, a texting companion that uses artificial intelligence technology to help you write, understand and reflect on messages;
  • Start a free trial of, purchase or manage an Emigo Pro subscription; or
  • Contact us for support or to exercise your privacy rights.

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. We are responsible for making decisions about how your personal information is processed. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at support@joinemigo.com or by post at Emigo Wellness, Inc., Attn: Support, 131 Continental Drive, Suite 305, Newark, DE 19713, United States.

Summary of Key Points

This summary provides key points from our Privacy Notice. You can find more details about any of these topics in the sections that follow. If this summary appears to conflict with the detailed sections, the detailed sections control.

  • Emigo does not read everything you type. Autocorrect and word learning work on your device. Your everyday typing is not sent to us.
  • Content is processed only when you use a feature. When you use an AI feature, the text or screenshots you choose to submit are sent securely through our server to our AI provider to produce your result. Your messages are never stored or sold by Emigo, and they are not used to train AI models.
  • Your history stays on your device. Learned words, typing data, feature history, chats, profile details and Insights are stored on your device, not on our servers.
  • Limited analytics, which you can turn off. We use PostHog for usage analytics, such as which features are used. Analytics are on by default and never include your text, chats or screenshots. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Emigo Pro is purchased through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status. We never receive your card details.
  • No accounts, no ads, no sale. You do not need an account to use Emigo. We do not sell your personal information, do not share it for targeted advertising, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live, you have rights to access, correct, delete and receive a copy of your personal information, and to complain to a regulator. See Sections 14 to 19.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

In Short: We collect only the information needed to provide, secure and improve Emigo. Most of your information stays on your device.

1.1 Content you choose to submit to a feature

When you use an AI feature, such as Reframe, Interpret, Create, Reply, Ask Emigo, Chat with Emigo, Unpack, Insights or Cool Down, the text, screenshots, images or questions that you choose to submit ("Inputs") are transmitted securely through our server to our AI provider so that it can generate a response ("Outputs"). Depending on the feature, the request may also include context needed to answer it, for example the earlier messages in a Chat conversation, the Mode you selected, or profile details you have entered in the app (such as the name you want Emigo to use and preferences you have set). Inputs and Outputs are processed only to provide the feature you requested. They are never stored or sold by Emigo, and they are not used for advertising, profiling or analytics. See Section 7.

Your Inputs may include information about other people, such as the messages they sent you or names that appear in a screenshot. We process that information only to provide the feature you requested.

1.2 Information that stays on your device

The following information is created and stored on your device, in storage that belongs to the Emigo app and keyboard. It is not uploaded to our servers:

  • Learned words and typing data used by autocorrect and word suggestions, so that the keyboard can adapt to the way you type;
  • Keyboard and app settings, including your chosen Modes and preferences;
  • Feature history, such as your recent rewrites and saved results;
  • Chats with Emigo and your profile details;
  • Insights, once they have been generated and returned to your device;
  • Emcognito codes, which are kept in your device's secure storage; and
  • Text you recently pasted into an Emigo tool from the keyboard.

Some of this information is sent to our AI provider when it is needed for a feature you use, as described in Section 1.1 and Section 7, but it is not kept by us. You can erase much of this information using the options in the app's Settings, and you can remove the app's stored data by deleting the app from your device. Some items held in iOS's secure Keychain, such as Emcognito codes, may remain on your device after the app is deleted until you reset your device.

1.3 Information you provide to us directly

If you email us for support, to give feedback or to make a privacy request, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, the content of your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to you and to keep a record of our correspondence.

1.4 Information collected automatically

In Short: We automatically collect limited technical and usage information when you use the app, as described below. It never includes the content of your messages.

  • Usage analytics. If analytics are turned on (the default), the app sends usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog, such as when the app is opened, which screens and features are used, whether a request succeeded, and settings you change. Events are linked to a random analytics ID created by the app (shared by the app and the keyboard), and include technical context such as your app version, iOS version, device model, screen size and language. Keyboard events about autocorrect contain only summary values, such as whether a suggestion was accepted and the approximate length of a word, never the word itself. Analytics never include your text, chats or screenshots. You can turn analytics off at any time. See Section 6.
  • Technical and security information. When the app connects to our server, our server and our hosting provider process your IP address, an installation identifier created using Apple's App Attest security service, the type of request, and the time and result of the request. We use this to deliver the Service, confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app, apply daily usage limits, and prevent fraud and abuse. Our server does not log the content of your requests.
  • Approximate location. We do not collect precise location. An approximate region, such as country or state, may be derived from your IP address by us or our service providers for security, fraud prevention and to understand where the Service is used.

1.5 Subscription and purchase information

If you start a free trial or buy Emigo Pro, Apple processes your payment. We receive subscription and purchase information from Apple and from our subscription management provider, RevenueCat, such as the product you purchased, whether your subscription or free trial is active, the purchase, renewal and expiry dates, the store transaction identifiers, the price and currency, and your country or region. We never receive your payment card number or bank details. See Section 9.

1.6 Device permissions

Some features ask for permission to use parts of your device. You can grant or withdraw these permissions at any time in your iOS Settings:

  • Full Access for the keyboard, which lets the keyboard connect to the internet to use the AI features. See Section 8.
  • Photos, only when you choose to attach a screenshot or image, using the iOS photo picker. We receive only the images you select, not your photo library.
  • Microphone and speech recognition, if you use voice typing. Voice typing uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. We do not receive your audio.
  • Face ID, if you choose to lock Emcognito or your chat history. Face ID checks are performed by iOS. We do not receive any biometric information.
  • Notifications, if you allow them. Emigo's reminders, such as keyboard setup tips, Cool Down check-ins, weekly reflections and free trial reminders, are scheduled on your device. We do not use a push notification service.
  • Clipboard. When you open an Emigo tool from the keyboard, the keyboard may check whether you copied text in the last two minutes so that it can offer to paste it into the tool. This text stays on your device unless you choose to submit it to a feature.

1.7 Information from our website

When you visit joinemigo.com, our website host and its content delivery network process your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, in order to deliver and secure the website. If you send us a message through a form on our website, we receive the information you enter.

2. What Information Do We Not Collect?

In Short: We do not collect your everyday typing, and we do not store your messages.

Emigo does not:

  • send your everyday typing, or a log of your keystrokes, to us;
  • store or sell the messages, text or screenshots you submit to a feature;
  • access your messages or conversations in other apps, except the text or screenshots you choose to submit;
  • require an account, your name, your email address, your phone number or a social-media login;
  • access your contacts or your photo library (other than images you select);
  • collect precise location information;
  • receive your audio or biometric data;
  • receive your payment card or bank details;
  • use advertising identifiers, show advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites; or
  • use your content to train AI models.

iOS does not allow third-party keyboards, including Emigo, to be used in secure fields such as password fields.

3. How Do We Process Your Information?

In Short: We process your information only as necessary to provide, secure, maintain and improve our Services, respond to you, and comply with the law.

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To provide the features you request. We process your Inputs and the related context to generate Outputs and return them to your device.
  • To provide and manage Emigo Pro. We process subscription information to unlock paid features, apply free trials, and confirm that your subscription is active.
  • To protect our Services. We process technical and security information to confirm that requests come from genuine copies of the app, to apply usage limits, and to detect, prevent and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents and technical problems.
  • To understand and improve our Services. If analytics are turned on, we use usage events to understand which features are used, measure performance and reliability, find and fix problems, and decide what to build next.
  • To respond to you. We use the information you send us to answer questions, provide support, and handle privacy requests.
  • To send reminders you have allowed. The app schedules local notifications on your device, such as free trial reminders.
  • To comply with legal obligations, to enforce our Terms of Service, and to protect the rights, property and safety of our users, Emigo and others.

We do not use your content for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models. We do not use your information to make decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Do We Use Cookies, Analytics And Other Tracking Technologies?

In Short: We use limited product analytics in the app, which you can turn off. We do not use advertising tracking.

App analytics. The Emigo app uses PostHog to collect the usage events described in Section 1.4. Analytics help us understand which features are useful, find problems and improve the Service. Analytics are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings using the "Share usage analytics" switch. When analytics are off, the app stops sending usage events. The app may continue to download feature settings from PostHog, which are used to switch features on or off, and this request involves your IP address and the random analytics ID.

What analytics never include. Analytics never include the text you type, your Inputs or Outputs, your chats, your screenshots or your learned words. We do not use the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

Website. Our website uses only the cookies and similar technologies that are necessary to deliver and secure the site. We do not use advertising cookies, remarketing or cross-site profiling, and we do not permit third parties to use cookies on our website for targeted advertising.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Because we do not sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or track you across third-party websites, there is nothing for a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal to switch off. If you send a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, to the extent applicable law requires.

7. Do We Offer Artificial Intelligence-Based Products?

In Short: Yes. Content you submit to a feature is processed by our AI provider to produce your result. It is never stored or sold by Emigo and is not used to train AI models.

Our AI Products. As part of our Services, we offer features powered by artificial intelligence (collectively, "AI Products"), designed to help you reframe, interpret, create and reply to messages and ask questions. They include Reframe, Interpret, Create, Reply, Ask Emigo, Chat with Emigo, Unpack, Insights and Cool Down. The Modes available in the app are Default, Work, Playful, Connections and Emcognito. The terms in this Privacy Notice govern how your information is processed when you use AI Products.

How your content is processed. When you use an AI Product, your Input and any related context described in Section 1.1 are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which is hosted by Cloudflare. Our server checks that the request comes from a genuine copy of the app, applies usage limits, and forwards the request to our AI provider, OpenAI. OpenAI generates the Output, which is returned through our server to your device. Our server does not store or log the content of your request or the Output.

No storage by Emigo, no sale, no training. Emigo never stores or sells your messages, Inputs or Outputs on its servers, and does not use them to train, fine-tune or evaluate AI models. Our AI provider is a third party. Its processing of the content sent to it is governed by its own API terms and privacy policy, which may permit it to retain content for a limited period, for example to detect and prevent abuse of its service. Our requests ask our AI provider not to store the content for later use, and we have not permitted it to use the content to train its models.

Insights and Chat. Insights are generated by our AI provider from the information on your device that is needed to produce them, and the result is stored on your device. Chat with Emigo sends your message, recent messages in the same conversation and relevant profile details to produce each reply. Your chat history is stored on your device.

Ask Emigo and web search. Ask Emigo may use our AI provider's web search capability to answer questions about current events or other information. When it does, your question, or search terms derived from it, may be sent to a search service through our AI provider.

Emcognito. Emcognito lets you protect a message with encryption on your device, using a key derived from a code that is stored in your device's secure storage. We do not receive or keep your codes or your protected messages. If you ask Emcognito to help create an ordinary-looking cover message, the visible conversation you provide is sent to our AI provider to write it, but your protected message is not.

Your responsibility for other people's information. If you submit messages or screenshots that include information about other people, please submit only what you need, and do not submit sensitive information, passwords or payment card details. See our Terms of Service.

8. Does Emigo Require Full Access?

Yes, for the AI features. iOS requires a third-party keyboard to have "Full Access" before it can connect to the internet or share information with its own app. Autocorrect and word learning work without sending your typing anywhere. Emigo uses Full Access only to:

  • send content you choose to submit to a feature, and receive the result;
  • share your settings, learned words and history between the Emigo keyboard and the Emigo app on your device;
  • paste text you have just copied into an Emigo tool, as described in Section 1.6; and
  • send usage analytics, if analytics are turned on.

Although iOS allows keyboards with Full Access to transmit what is typed, Emigo does not send your everyday typing to us or to anyone else. You can turn Full Access off at any time in iOS Settings, in which case some features will not be available.

9. Emigo Pro Subscriptions (Apple App Store and RevenueCat)

In Short: Emigo Pro is a paid subscription sold through the App Store and managed with RevenueCat. We receive subscription status, not your payment details.

  • Billing is handled by Apple. Payment, including after a free trial, is charged to your Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy applies to the information Apple collects when you make a purchase.
  • Subscription management with RevenueCat. We use RevenueCat to confirm and keep track of your subscription and free trial. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app user ID that is created from the app's security key on your device, together with your purchase and transaction information from Apple (such as the product, transaction identifiers, price, currency, store, and purchase, renewal and expiry dates) and basic technical information (such as country, app version and platform). We do not give RevenueCat your name or email address.
  • No card details. Neither Emigo nor RevenueCat receives your card number, security code or bank details.
  • Managing your subscription. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.

We use this information to provide Emigo Pro, to apply free trials fairly, to prevent unauthorized use of paid features, to keep financial records, and to understand subscription trends. We do not use it to advertise to you.

11. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

In Short: We protect your information with technical and organizational security measures, and we keep as little of it as possible.

We have implemented appropriate and reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the personal information we process, including:

  • encryption of all connections between the app, our server and our service providers;
  • encryption of personal data that the app stores on your device, together with iOS data protection;
  • Apple's App Attest service, so that our server accepts requests only from genuine copies of the app;
  • not storing or logging the content of your requests on our server;
  • keeping Emcognito codes in your device's secure storage; and
  • limiting access to personal information to the people and service providers who need it.

However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that hackers, cybercriminals or other unauthorized third parties will not be able to defeat our security and improperly collect, access, steal or modify your information. Much of your information is stored on your device, so please protect your device with a passcode and keep iOS up to date. If we become aware of a security breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant regulators where required by law.

12. Is Your Information Transferred Internationally?

In Short: Yes. We are based in the United States, and your information is processed there.

Emigo Wellness, Inc. is based in the United States, and our service providers process information primarily in the United States. Our hosting provider operates a global network, so a request may pass through a data center close to you before it reaches the United States. If you live in Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia or New Zealand, your personal information will be transferred to, and processed in, the United States and possibly other countries where our service providers operate. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from the laws of your country, and while your information is there, it may be accessible to the courts, law enforcement and national security authorities of those countries under their laws.

Content you submit to a feature is transferred to our AI provider in the United States because the transfer is necessary to provide the feature you request under our Terms of Service. By using the AI features, you acknowledge that your content will be transferred and processed in this way.

13. Do We Collect Information From Minors?

In Short: Emigo is for people aged 13 and over. Users under 18 need the agreement of a parent or guardian.

Emigo is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in accordance with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. If you are under 13, please do not use Emigo or provide any personal information.

Users aged 13 to 17, or under the age of majority where they live, may use Emigo only with the agreement of a parent or legal guardian. Where a higher minimum age applies under the law of your country, province or state, that age applies. We do not use the personal information of users under 18 for targeted advertising, and we do not sell it.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it. Parents or guardians who believe that their child under 13 has provided information to us may contact support@joinemigo.com to request deletion. Because most information stays on the device, parents and guardians can also remove it by deleting the app from the child's device.

14. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

In Short: Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict how we use it.

Most of your information is stored on your device, where you can view and delete it yourself. For personal information that we hold, depending on where you live and subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • know whether we process your personal information, and access it;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information;
  • delete your personal information;
  • receive a copy of your personal information in a portable format;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw your consent where we rely on consent; and
  • complain to a data protection regulator.

Sections 15 to 19 describe rights that apply in particular countries and states. You can exercise your rights by contacting us as described in Section 23. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Withdrawing your consent. If we rely on your consent to process your information, you may withdraw it at any time by changing the relevant setting (such as "Share usage analytics" or your iOS permissions) or by contacting us. This will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before your withdrawal.
