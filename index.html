Cool Down catches the moment. Later, Emigo checks in.

Cool Down: the keyboard ices over.

When you're typing something heated, Cool Down frosts the keys and starts a short timer. Give it a breath, tap "Help me say it better", or tap "I've got this". You still decide what goes out.

later that day

Emigo Chat: a check-in afterwards.

With Cool Down on, Emigo can check in later. Open it and Chat already knows there was a hot moment, so you don't have to retell it. Walk it through, or say "Not tonight". Chat is there any other time too, for whatever you want to talk through.

Your everyday typing isn't sent to us.

The plain version is here. The privacy policy has the detail.

Emigo can't see your conversations.
iOS only lets a keyboard see the text box you're typing in. Your messages, inboxes and chat history in Messages, WhatsApp, Instagram or any other app are off-limits to Emigo, and to every keyboard. Emigo sees a message only when you choose to give it one, by pasting it or a screenshot into a tool.
Your Emigo Chat history stays on your phone.
Your conversations with Emigo and your chat history are stored on your device, not on our servers. To answer you, the conversation is sent securely to produce the reply, and Emigo doesn't keep it.
What Emigo learns stays on your phone.
Learned words and your history live on your device. Delete them anytime in Settings, or by deleting the app.
Never stored or sold by Emigo.
When you use Reframe, Interpret, Create or Chat, your text is sent securely to our AI provider to produce the result. Emigo doesn't store it, sell it, or use it to train models.
Analytics you can switch off.
Usage analytics (which features get used, never your text or chats) are on by default. Turn them off any time in Settings, under "Share usage analytics".

Fair questions.

Does Emigo read everything I type?

No. Your everyday typing is not sent to us. Text or screenshots are processed only when you choose to use a feature such as Reframe, Interpret or Create, and they are not stored on our servers.

Why does the keyboard ask for Full Access?

Full Access is the standard iOS permission that any keyboard with online features needs, the same one Grammarly, Gboard and SwiftKey ask for. Without it, no keyboard can connect to the internet. Emigo uses it to send what you choose to submit to a feature, to share your settings between the keyboard and the app, and for usage analytics, which you can turn off.

Is it free?

Emigo is free to download and you don't need an account. Free covers the keyboard, Default mode, Reframe, Interpret and Create in Default, and Cool Down. Work, Playful, Connections, Emcognito and In Your Voice come with Emigo Pro, a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription with a one-week free trial for eligible users. The prices are above, and the final price is shown in the App Store before you buy.

Does Emigo work in every app?

Yes. Emigo is a keyboard, so it works anywhere you type: Messages, WhatsApp, Instagram, Gmail, Slack and more. iOS switches to the standard keyboard in password fields and a few other secure fields, and some apps choose not to allow third-party keyboards.
